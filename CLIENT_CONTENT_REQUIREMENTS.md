### 11.2 IMAGE SPECIFICATION SUMMARY

| Image Type | Format | Minimum Size | Orientation | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hero / Banner | JPG or WebP | 1920 × 1080px | Landscape | High quality, well-lit |
| Hero Slider Slides | JPG or WebP | 1920 × 1080px | Landscape | Consistent across slides |
| Portfolio Project Photos | JPG or WebP | 1200 × 800px | Landscape | Professional photography preferred |
| Team / Founder Portraits | JPG or PNG | 600 × 750px | Portrait | Clear face, neutral background |
| Product Photos | JPG or WebP | 800 × 800px | Square or Portrait | Clean background for main shot |
| Blog / Article Cover | JPG or WebP | 1200 × 900px | Landscape (4:3) | Visually descriptive |
| Studio Gallery | JPG or WebP | 600 × 600px | Square or Landscape | Consistent style |
| Process Step Images | JPG or WebP | 800 × 600px | Landscape | Optional |
| Logo | PNG | 600px wide | — | **Transparent background mandatory** |
| Favicon | PNG | 512 × 512px | Square | — |
| OG / Social Share | JPG | 1200 × 630px | Landscape | Used by WhatsApp / Facebook |
| Videos | MP4 | 1080p HD | Landscape | Max 150MB, no audio needed |

### 11.3 COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

| ❌ Don't | ✅ Do instead |
|---|---|
| Send images via WhatsApp | Use Google Drive or WeTransfer |
| Send logo on a white box | Send PNG with transparent background |
| Send photos smaller than 800px | Send original uncompressed photos |
| Mix up file names | Name files exactly as listed in 11.1 |
| Send vertical video for hero | Send horizontal / landscape video |
| Send screenshots of photos | Send original JPG/PNG files |
| Use Google Photos links | Download files, put in Drive folder |
